Privacy Policy for Campus Connect

Last updated: 22/07/2026

Campus Connect ("the app") is developed by Syed Shahmir Shoaib as a community app for students to share campus announcements and lost & found items.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your roll number
  • Any content you post (announcements or lost & found listings)

How We Use This Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display your name alongside posts you create
  • To let other students identify who posted an announcement or lost/found item

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore Database and Firebase Authentication). We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Data Retention

Your account data and posts remain stored until you request deletion. To request account or data deletion, contact us at shahmirshoaib005@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact shahmirshoaib005@gmail.com.
